# Pack: PM documents (PRD, requirements, stories, minutes, updates)

## Intake questions

- Document type, audience, and the decision it supports.
- Source material: notes, transcripts, tickets, earlier versions. Exact files.
- Template or house format to follow. Language.
- What is decided, what is proposed, what is open.

## Rules

- Every requirement, decision, date, owner and number traces to a source line (note, transcript, ticket, user answer). No source: write `TBD — ask <who>`; never fill in a plausible owner or date.
- Keep three things apart: what was said, what was decided, what you infer. Inference is labelled.
- Requirements are testable: one behaviour each, with an acceptance criterion.
- New demands are written as trade-offs: what it costs and what moves.
- Meeting minutes default: Header, Overview, Detailed Notes, Action Points (owner, due date, source line).
- Outline is approved at G2 before prose is written. Standard tier writes; light tier fills tables from sources.
- Verifier checks a sample of statements against the sources and lists anything it cannot find.

## Acceptance checks

Traceability table complete · no unlabelled inference · all TBDs listed as questions · format matches template.
